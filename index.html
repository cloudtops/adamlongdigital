How it goes.

Process

Four steps, two revision rounds, one person on the whole thing.

01

Brief

Thirty minutes on the business, who you want reaching, and what you want them to do. Photos, video, and brand files get collected here.

02

Story

Copy and structure come first. You sign off on the words before any design work starts.

03

Build

Design and development run together on a private staging link. Two rounds of revisions are included in every project.

04

Launch

Accounts connected, assets delivered, inquiries pointed at your inbox, and reporting set up so month two starts with numbers.
