Services

4 lines

Take one line or all four. Most people start with social and add the rest once they can see it working.

01

Social media management

Your accounts, run properly. I build the monthly calendar, make the posts and Reels out of media you already have, write the captions in your voice, and send you a report that says what actually moved.

Content calendar, graphics and carousels, Reels and vertical cuts, captions, scheduling, monthly reporting.

02

Brand and identity

The look everything else gets built on. Wordmark, colors, and type, plus the templates that keep it consistent once other people start using it.

Wordmark, visual identity, brand guidelines, social templates, decks, print collateral.

03

Websites

Custom sites, no templates. Business and personal-brand sites, campaign landing pages, and the single-property listing sites I am known for.

Business sites, portfolio sites, landing pages, listing microsites, copywriting, domain and analytics.

04

Campaigns and email

The part that reaches people who do not already follow you. Email sequences, list setup, paid targeting, and weekly numbers you can read without needing a call.

Email design, sequences, list segmentation, paid targeting, budget management, weekly reporting.

Process
  1. Brief. Thirty minutes on your business, who you want to reach, and what you want them to do. Send me whatever photos, video, and brand files you already have.
  2. Story. Copy and structure first. You sign off on the words before I design anything.
  3. Build. Design and development happen together on a private link you can watch. Two revision rounds included, always.
  4. Launch. Accounts connected, files delivered, inquiries pointed at your inbox, reporting set up so month two starts with real numbers.
